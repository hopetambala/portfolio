I watch Survivor with my wife and at some point just keeping score in our heads stopped working. We needed a real tracker, one that could handle alliance predictions, tribal council outcomes, and who we each thought was going home next.

So I built one during a weekend. The app lets you follow a season in real time: log eliminations as they happen, submit predictions before each episode, and see how your pick stacks up against everyone else watching with you. It's the kind of thing that only makes sense to build if you care deeply about both the show and the excuse to write some code.

Full-stack with a database behind it, so the data persists between episodes instead of resetting every time someone refreshes the page. Small surface area, exactly as complicated as it needed to be.
