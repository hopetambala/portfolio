Commonplace is Cityblock Health's design system. I led design and engineering for the system that powers care-delivery tooling used by clinicians and care teams in a complex, high-stakes healthcare environment.

That meant pairing closely with design in Figma, building a robust React/TypeScript component library, and wiring it to live data through GraphQL — all while holding a high bar for accessibility and reliability where the stakes are people's health.
